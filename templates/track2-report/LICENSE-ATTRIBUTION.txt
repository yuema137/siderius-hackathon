GALILEO ADAPTATION
main.tex replaces the example paper content with Galileo Hackathon 2026 report
prompts, uses the single-blind workshop option, sets the workshop title and
overrides only the venue notice. It preserves the official font sizes, margins
and spacing. README.txt supplies Galileo-specific instructions. preview.pdf is
a compilation of this adapted main.tex. The additional template text is made
available under the same CC BY 4.0 terms, attributed to Galileo Hackathon 2026
report template contributors. Scientific content later added by participants
is not assigned a license by this notice.
